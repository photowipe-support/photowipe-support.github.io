Privacy Policy

PhotoWipe · Last updated September 27, 2026

Overview

PhotoWipe is designed to help you review and organize the photos and videos in your photo library. Your privacy is important to us, and PhotoWipe is designed to perform its core photo-management functionality on your device.

Photo Library Access

PhotoWipe requests access to your photo library so that you can view, organize, keep, and delete photos and videos.

PhotoWipe does not upload your photos or videos to servers operated by PhotoWipe. Photo and video processing performed by PhotoWipe occurs on your device.

Some photos or videos may be stored in iCloud and accessed through Apple's Photos services according to your device and iCloud settings.

Data Collection

PhotoWipe does not require an account and does not collect personal information, analytics data, advertising data, or tracking data through the PhotoWipe app.

Local Data

PhotoWipe may store information locally on your device to remember app state and your photo-management progress. This information is used to provide the app's functionality and is not transmitted to PhotoWipe servers.

Third-Party Services

PhotoWipe does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics services.

Support

If you choose to contact PhotoWipe Support, you may voluntarily provide information such as your email address and the contents of your support request. Support requests are submitted using Google Forms and are therefore also subject to Google's applicable privacy practices.

You can contact support through the PhotoWipe Support page.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if PhotoWipe's features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
